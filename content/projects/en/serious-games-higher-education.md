Planning a project, dealing with stakeholders, making decisions under uncertainty: much of what project managers need is learned through experience rather than in lectures.

### The gap

In university courses, students mainly learn methods and tools. Practical competencies and soft skills remain hard to acquire, and students have few safe opportunities to try, fail and try again.

### Project objectives

Building on ten years of experience with the PM-Game, the project had three aims:

1. **Explore:** assess the potential of serious games for educational innovation in university education.
2. **Test:** integrate serious games into university courses and observe how they are used in class.
3. **Share:** disseminate the results to the academic community.
