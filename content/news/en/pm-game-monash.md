title: 'The PM-Game in Australia: first use at Monash University'
date: 2026-06-01
image: ../../../src/assets/news/pm-game-monash.jpg
---

In Melbourne, Monash University has incorporated our PM-Game into its curriculum. This is the first use of the simulation on the Australian continent. Developed and maintained for over 10 years by AlbaSim, the PM-Game allows learners to experience project management in a simulated environment. This collaboration illustrates the growing interest in educational simulations beyond Europe.
