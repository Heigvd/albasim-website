The synthesis report of the National Research Programme “Digital Transformation” (NRP 77) compiles the findings of six years of research conducted across Switzerland, assessing the opportunities, challenges and impacts of digital transformation on society, the economy and education. AlbaSim took part as project leader of the co.LAB project, conducted with the University of Geneva, which led to the development, implementation and evaluation of the co.LAB Serious Games Design Framework. The report details the policy, economic and societal recommendations arising from the projects supported by the SNSF.
