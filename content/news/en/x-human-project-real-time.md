Multi-patient emergency situations are common in hospitals, yet existing simulations do not support training or research on these situations. x-HUMAn builds on HUMAn, a real-time patient simulation model developed by AlbaSim and the Geneva University Hospitals (HUG) in the MIMMS project ([Laurent et al., JMIR Formative Research, 2025](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40053123/)). HUMAn can already simulate several patients in real time. The x-HUMAn project extends HUMAn by integrating pharmacology and the response to treatment. HEIG-VD provides its expertise in engineering and simulation; the HUG provide medical expertise and validation in hospital settings. Lasting three years and funded by Innosuisse, the project will develop a simulation to be used for research and training in the management of multi-patient situations.
