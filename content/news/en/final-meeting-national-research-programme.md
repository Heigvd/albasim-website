NRP 77 generated knowledge about the opportunities, risks and challenges of digitalisation in Switzerland. We took part with co.LAB, a four-year project on digital learning games. The final meeting was an opportunity to share our results and recommendations with the steering committee and to exchange with other research teams of the programme.
