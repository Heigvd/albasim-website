At the Journée Technique Romande for advanced medical posts, Xavier Good (AlbaSim) and Eric Golay (HUG) led five sessions in which doctors, nurses, midwives, paramedics and first responders tested our pre-triage simulation. Initially designed for individual use, the tool showed its full potential in group work: debates and comparisons of viewpoints enriched the reflection on triage criteria and revealed differences in reasoning across professions. Individual and collective uses proved complementary.
