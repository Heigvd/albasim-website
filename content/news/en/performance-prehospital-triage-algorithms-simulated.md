Based on our physiological simulation model and our Mass Casualties Triage simulation, a study evaluated the performance of two triage algorithms. Results are published in Prehospital and Disaster Medicine. Participants were randomly assigned the simple Sieve algorithm or the more detailed SwissPre approach, and asked to triage 30 simulated patients in a fire-related incident. Sieve slightly outperformed SwissPre in prioritising critically injured patients, with no meaningful difference in triage time. Because the study used a controlled simulation, results should be interpreted cautiously and call for further research in real settings.
