The continuing education day for paramedics and emergency medical directors is organised by GRIMCA and the Service d’Incendie et de Secours des Montagnes Neuchâteloises. Simon Regard (HUG), Eric Golay (HUG) and Dominique Jaccard (AlbaSim) used the Major Incident Medical Simulation to lead four training sessions. Feedback from the 38 participants was very positive about the relevance of the simulation as a training tool.
